Rotate, air,
repeat.

Three habits that add years to any mattress — ours or anyone else's — and one that matters more in Nepal than almost anywhere.

02 · Care  ·  3 min read

A mattress does not usually fail. It wears unevenly, in the two places where you lie, until one day the middle feels like a ridge and your back tells you about it. Almost all of that is preventable, and none of it takes more than a few minutes a season.

1. Rotate every three months

Turn the mattress end to end — head where the feet were. Do not flip it over unless it is a two-sided build; single-sided mattresses have their comfort layers on top for a reason. Rotating spreads the wear across the whole surface, and four times a year is enough. Put it in your phone for the first of Baisakh, Shrawan, Kartik and Magh and you will never think about it again.

2. Air it, especially in monsoon

Humidity is the real enemy here. Through Asar and Shrawan the air in a closed bedroom can sit above 80% for weeks, and a mattress under a fitted sheet has nowhere to send the moisture it absorbs from you every night. Once a week, pull the sheets off, open the windows and let the surface breathe for a couple of hours. On a dry day after the rains, stand the mattress on its side against a wall for half a day. It is the single most useful thing you can do for it.

Never dry a mattress in direct sun for hours. It yellows covers, breaks down foam and does nothing that shade and moving air would not do better.

3. Use a protector, wash the protector

A washable protector takes the sweat, the spilled tea and the child who was sure they did not need to go. Wash it monthly at 40 °C. A mattress that never meets a liquid will outlast one that does by years, and no warranty in the country covers staining.

The small things

  • Do not let anyone jump on it — springs deform where they are shocked, not where they are loaded.
  • Carry it flat or on its side; never bend or fold a spring mattress to get it up a stairwell.
  • Vacuum the surface with the upholstery head every few months, gently.
  • Check the base once a year. A sagging bed frame will ruin a good mattress faster than any amount of use.

If something has already gone wrong — a dip deeper than about 3 cm with nothing on the bed, a seam opening, a spring you can feel — that is a warranty conversation, not a care one. Bring us a photograph and we will tell you honestly which it is.
