— Our story

More than two
decades of expertise,
now in Nepal.

Jagriti began with a single quilting machine and a simple promise: that a good night should not have to be imported.

— Where it started

One machine,
one rented room.

In 2003 our founder was repairing mattresses that had been shipped in, opened up, and found wanting. The foam was thinner than the label said. The springs sang. Families were paying import prices for something no one here would have signed off on.

So he bought a quilting machine, rented a room in Indrayani, and started making them properly.

— Twenty-two years

How it grew.

2003

The first quilting machine

Two people, one machine and a rented room in Indrayani. The first year's output was 340 mattresses, all sold within the valley.

2009

We start winding our own springs

Buying spring units meant accepting someone else's gauge. The coiling line changed what we could promise about support — and how long it would last.

2015

Rebuilding after the earthquake

The floor was back running in eleven days, and for three months a share of everything we made went to families in Sindhupalchok.

2021

The Core Plus range

A tempered bonnell spring core built to sit beside anything imported — at a price that made sense to buy here.

Today

Same family, same floor

Two mattress lines in production, 64 people on the payroll, and the founder still walks the line most mornings.

2003
— What has not changed

Still a family floor.

The same family runs the workshop, the same hands check every roll of foam, and the standard has only gone up. We are not trying to be the biggest mattress company in Nepal. We would rather be the one people name when a neighbour asks.

— What we hold to

Three quiet rules.

01 · Honest materials

The label is the mattress.

Stated density is real density. If we cannot trace it, it does not go in.

02 · Fair work

Paid on time, every time.

Sixty-four people, salaried, with the same holidays whether the season is busy or slow.

03 · Local first

Made where it is sold.

Cotton, felt and covers sourced in Nepal wherever the quality is there to do it.
