— How it works

Three days,
three steps.

Everything in the collection is made to order in Sankharapur, so nothing sits in a warehouse waiting for you.

01

We confirm

A call within a few hours of the order to check the size, the address and which day suits you.

02

We build it

Your mattress is made and inspected on our floor. Most take two working days; a custom size takes three.

03

We carry it in

Two people, a two-hour window, set up on your frame. Packaging and the old mattress leave with us.
