— Warranty

A warranty is
a promise, not
a sticker.

Both mattresses are covered against sagging, spring or coir failure and manufacturing faults. We repair first, replace where we cannot, and we come to you.

— Cover by range

How long you
are covered.

Cover runs from the delivery date, not the order date, and the first two years include collection and return at our cost.

— Royal

7 years

Against sagging over 3 cm, pocketed spring failure, and seam or stitching faults.

— Core Plus

5 years

Against sagging over 3 cm, spring failure, and seam or stitching faults.

— Rebonded, foam & latex

5 years

Against sagging over 3 cm, rebonded foam that stops recovering, and seam faults.

— Coir lines

3 years

Against sagging over 3 cm, coir separation, and seam or stitching faults.

Memory foam, high-density foam and natural latex lines carry five years on the same terms. Quilts, quilt covers, pillows and toppers carry one year against stitching and filling faults — normal flattening with use is not a fault. A pillow top or euro top does not change the cover on the mattress underneath it.

— What is covered

Faults we built in.

  • Sagging deeper than 3 cm on a properly supported base
  • Broken, loose or noisy springs
  • Seams, stitching and tape edges coming apart
  • Comfort foam that no longer recovers its shape
  • Coir layers separating or breaking up on the coir lines
— What is not

Wear, damage and taste.

  • Normal body impressions under 3 cm
  • Stains, burns, tears and water damage
  • Damage from an unsupported or broken bed frame
  • Mattresses used in hostels, hotels or rentals
  • Deciding it is firmer or softer than you hoped
  • Any mattress that has been altered or re-covered
— Making a claim

Three steps,
no forms.

01

Send a photograph

The mattress stripped bare, plus the law label on the side. Email or message it to us.

02

We come and measure

Within the valley, someone visits within three working days. Elsewhere we work from photographs and a straight edge.

03

Repair or replace

Most faults are repaired on our floor within a week. Where they cannot be, you get a replacement of the same model.
