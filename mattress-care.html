— Mattress care

Look after it
and it will look
after you.

A mattress rarely fails outright — it wears unevenly, in the two places you lie. Four habits, four times a year, and most of that never happens.

— The whole routine

Four habits,
ten minutes each.

Put them in your phone for the first of Baisakh, Shrawan, Kartik and Magh and you will never think about it again.

Rotate

End to end every three months. Do not flip it unless it is a two-sided build — ask us if you are not sure which yours is.

Air

Sheets off, windows open, a couple of hours a week. After the rains, stand it on its side for half a day.

Protect

A washable protector, washed monthly at 40 °C. No warranty in the country covers staining.

Check the base

Once a year. A sagging frame ruins a good mattress faster than any amount of sleeping on it.

DRY
— The monsoon rule

Humidity is the
real enemy here.

Through Asar and Shrawan a closed bedroom can sit above 80% humidity for weeks, and a mattress under a fitted sheet has nowhere to send what it absorbs from you every night. Air it weekly, and never dry it in direct sun — shade and moving air do the same job without yellowing the cover or breaking down the foam.

— Worth doing

Small things that help.

  • Vacuum the surface gently with the upholstery head every few months
  • Carry it flat or on its side, never folded
  • Blot spills immediately with a dry cloth, then let it air fully
  • Use a slatted base with gaps under 7 cm, or a solid ventilated one
  • Keep the space under the bed clear enough for air to move
— Worth avoiding

Things that shorten it.

  • Letting anyone jump on it — springs deform where they are shocked
  • Sitting on the same edge every day to put shoes on
  • Hours of direct sun “to kill the damp”
  • Steam cleaning, or soaking a stain to get it out
  • Leaving it wrapped in its delivery plastic
