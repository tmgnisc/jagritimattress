— What to expect

Half an hour,
no pressure.

Wear something comfortable, and bring whoever shares the bed with you. It matters more than you would think.

01

Tell us how you sleep

Side, back, hot, restless, sore in the morning — five minutes of questions tells us which of the two suits you, and in which size.

02

Lie on all three

Ten minutes each, in the position you actually sleep in. We will leave you alone for it.

03

Sleep on the decision

Nothing is held back and nothing sells out overnight. Go home, talk it over, come back.
