— What makes the difference

Four things we
refuse to outsource.

Most of what decides how a mattress feels in year five is decided in the first hour of making it.

Springs wound here

Bonnell and pocket coils are wound and tempered on our own line, so gauge and tension stay consistent across a whole batch.

Foam we can trace

High-resilience foam at a stated density, cut in-house. No mystery offcuts, no re-bonded filler hiding under the quilt.

Our own quilting

Covers are stitched on our quilting machines and tape-edged by hand, which is why the seams stay square after years of use.

Checked twice

Every mattress is inspected when it leaves the line and again before it goes on the delivery truck. Two signatures, every time.

— Tested for here

Built for Nepali
homes and nights.

An imported mattress is designed for someone else's climate. Ours are rolled, pressed and slept on through a Kathmandu monsoon before they ever reach a showroom.

  • Humidity testing through Asar and Shrawan
  • 30,000-cycle roller press on every new build
  • Edge-support load tested at 90 kg
  • Cover shrinkage checked after three washes

— Inside every layer

Six layers,
top to base.

This is the Core Plus build. The coir and rebonded lines follow the same order without the spring unit, layering natural coir in its place.

01

Knit cover

Breathable stretch knit, tape-edged by hand so the corners keep their shape.

02

Quilted comfort layer

Soft foam quilted into the cover — the first inch you actually feel.

03

Resilient foam

High-resilience foam that takes the edge off the springs without going soft.

04

Cotton felt

An insulating pad that keeps the springs from reading through the foam.

05

Bonnell spring unit

Tempered coils wound and knotted on our own line, firmer through the middle third.

06

Edge support frame

A firmer perimeter so you can sit on the side without rolling off it.
